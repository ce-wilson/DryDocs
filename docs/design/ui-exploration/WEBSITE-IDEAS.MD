## 🔵 Logo Concepts for *DryDocs*
Your original red‑sphere‑with‑orbiting‑rectangles is actually a strong foundation. The images you uploaded lean toward **data flow**, **graph networks**, **interconnected nodes**, and **abstract computational structures** — perfect for a knowledge graph product.

Here are three distinct logo directions that stay true to your original concept but modernize it.

---

### **1. “The Core + Orbit” (Modernized Original)**
**Concept:**  
A central sphere (DryDocs core knowledge) with orbiting rectangular “tiles” representing reusable documentation blocks.

**Visual cues:**  
- Sphere stays red (brand anchor).  
- Orbiting tiles become **flat, glassy, or semi‑transparent panels** in DevOps colors: teal, blue, green, orange.  
- Panels arranged in a **graph‑like arc**, not a full circle — suggests flow rather than containment.  
- Subtle motion lines or gradients to imply automation.

**Meaning:**  
Reusable knowledge blocks orbiting a single source of truth.

---

### **2. “The Knowledge Graph Node”**
**Concept:**  
A hexagon or cube cluster (like your sample images) with one highlighted “root node.”

**Visual cues:**  
- Central node in red.  
- Surrounding nodes in muted blues/greens.  
- Thin connection lines forming a **mini‑graph**.  
- Slight 3D depth but still flat enough for modern UI.

**Meaning:**  
DryDocs is the authoritative node that connects everything — jobs, servers, lineage, teams.

---

### **3. “The DevOps Pipeline Loop”**
**Concept:**  
A circular loop (CI/CD pipeline) intersecting with a graph node.

**Visual cues:**  
- Loop in two colors (Dev + Ops).  
- A red sphere sits at the intersection point.  
- Small squares or dots along the loop represent jobs, servers, or steps.

**Meaning:**  
DryDocs sits at the intersection of DevOps automation and knowledge.
