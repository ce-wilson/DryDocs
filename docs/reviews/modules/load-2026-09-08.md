### One non-finding, recorded so it is not re-raised

`drydocs.cli_consumer` is declared in `COMPONENT_GROUPS['load']` and **no such module
exists in this tree** — `import drydocs.cli_consumer` raises `ModuleNotFoundError`. That
is correct and deliberate: S16 / RELAY-24 made `cli_consumer.py` the CONSUMER's optional
composition surface, shipped by a consumer tree (the company port, a Team Edition
instance) and never by the producer. `tests/unit/test_cli_import_order.py:44` adds it to
`CLI_MODULES` only `if importlib.util.find_spec(...) is not None`, and
`test_cli_registry.py` states the producer ships none. This is exactly the shape
`docs/style/review-provenance.md` warns about — absent here reads as not-yet-ported, not
as broken — and it cost one lookup to confirm, which is cheaper than the finding it would
otherwise have become.
